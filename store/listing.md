**App name** (30): Menu Admin

**Short description** (80):
Run your restaurant's digital menu from your phone: dishes, sold out, QR codes.

**Full description** (4000):
Menu Admin is the companion app for restaurants that publish their menu on our digital menu platform. Everything you do in the web admin, you can now do from your phone or tablet, even with a weak connection.

FOR BUSY SERVICE
• One-tap "Sold out" for any dish: guests see it within seconds
• A big Sold-out list made for the kitchen and the counter
• Works offline for reading: your menu opens instantly from your phone

YOUR MENU
• Menus, categories and dishes with sizes, add-ons, tags, allergens and calories
• Up to 5 photos per dish, from the camera or gallery, with cropping
• Drag to reorder, bulk price changes, duplicate dishes
• Branches with their own prices and availability
• Happy-hour and lunch prices, specials and announcements

LOOK AND SHARE
• Four menu styles, your brand colour, logo and banner, with a live preview of the real menu
• QR codes for the restaurant, each branch and every table
• Table tents, posters, stickers and social media pictures, ready to print or share
• Opening hours with breaks, holidays and closures

KNOW YOUR GUESTS
• Menu views, QR scans and most viewed dishes
• Export CSV or a PDF report; monthly report by e-mail

TEAM AND ACCOUNT
• Invite managers and staff with the right permissions
• Plan, paid-until date and invoices (PDF)
• Two-step sign-in, sign-in on several devices, optional fingerprint/PIN lock
• Notifications: plan ending, monthly report, new team member, platform news

FOR THE PLATFORM ADMIN
• Dashboard with growth, health and the restaurants that need attention
• Restaurants, approvals, payments and invoices, messages, backups and settings

Menu Admin needs an account on the platform. New restaurants sign up on the website.
